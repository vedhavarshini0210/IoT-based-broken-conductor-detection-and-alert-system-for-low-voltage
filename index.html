About This Demonstration

This web page is a front-end demonstration for an IoT-based broken conductor detection and alert system. The buttons change sample values locally in the browser; they do not connect to real sensors, electrical equipment, or an alert service. Actual electrical fault detection and response must be designed, tested, and handled by qualified professionals using suitable certified equipment.
